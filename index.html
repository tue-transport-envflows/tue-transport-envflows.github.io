Transport in Environmental Flows

“Understanding our surroundings is a crucial step towards improving them.”

We study how environmental flows transport material and substances — from sediment on the seabed to droplets in the air we breathe — combining laboratory experiments with numerical simulations across scales to turn fundamental understanding into better predictions.

About the group

The Transport in Environmental Flows group, led by Matias Duran Matute, is part of the Fluids and Flows research group in the Department of Applied Physics and Science Education at Eindhoven University of Technology. We investigate how material and substances are transported by geophysical and environmental flows — in coastal seas, estuaries, and indoor air — with the aim of improving predictions that support decision-makers.

Our approach combines controlled laboratory experiments using optical measurement techniques with numerical simulations spanning multiple length scales, bridging fundamental fluid dynamics and geophysical fluid mechanics with practical, real-world applications.

Research topics

Our work spans coastal, estuarine, and indoor environmental flows, linking fundamental transport processes to real-world impact.

🏖️

Sediment transport & morphodynamics

Incipient motion and transport of sediment in coastal areas, including the evolution of the seabed in systems such as the Dutch Wadden Sea.

🌊

Estuarine & exchange flows

Salt transport in estuaries, stratified turbulence, and exchange flows that govern mixing between fresh and saline water bodies.

🧭

Lagrangian transport & dispersion

Tidal and wind-driven dispersion of particles in intertidal basins, and the residual transport pathways of marine debris.

🌀

Wave-induced drift

The effect of the Earth's rotation and finite depth on wave-induced drift, and the resulting motion of floating and submerged particles.

🫧

Exhaled droplet transport

How respiratory droplets are transported and dispersed in indoor environments, relevant to air quality and disease transmission.

🏞️

Gravity currents & shallow flows

Propagation of gravity currents against counter flows, and the dynamics of shallow layers relevant to coastal and estuarine systems.

📷

Photogrammetry

Optical and photogrammetric techniques to measure sediment layers and flow fields in the laboratory with high spatial resolution.
